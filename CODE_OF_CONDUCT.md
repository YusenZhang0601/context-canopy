# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want ContextCanopy to be a respectful, evidence-oriented community where people can question designs, report mistakes, and disagree without personal attacks.

Positive participation includes:

- giving specific, technically useful feedback;
- separating observed evidence from assumptions;
- respecting privacy, consent, and project boundaries;
- welcoming contributors with different experience levels;
- correcting mistakes openly and proportionately.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing private data, sustained disruption, or sexualized conduct and imagery.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and whether harm is repeated.

Report conduct concerns privately to **zhangyswx@163.com**. Reports will be handled with discretion; do not include unrelated sensitive information.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
